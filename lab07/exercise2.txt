Checkoff Question 2:

Checkoff Question 3:

  The way we stride through matrices in the innermost loop directly affects performance because it
  determines how we exploit spatial and temporal locality. Iterating over contiguous memory
  locations (stride = 1) keeps cache lines hot and leads to higher hit rates, while large strides
  cause frequent cache misses and poorer performance.
